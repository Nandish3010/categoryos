# Outreach: real quotes from fellow category managers

## Rules (read first)
- Quotes must be **real**: only what the person actually wrote. Never edit meaning, invent, or paraphrase into a quote; light trimming only, and show them the final text.
- Attributable: get **name, role, and company type** (e.g. "Category Manager, mid-size pet e-commerce brand") and **explicit permission** to quote them. If they want anonymity, say "category manager at an Indian e-commerce company" only with their OK.
- Ask them **not to upload any confidential data** (real vendor names, costs, margins, price lists, contracts, documents). The app has synthetic samples (one-click on `/try` and V001/V002) that are enough.
- Do not pay, pressure, or offer anything for a positive reply. Include criticism in what you report.
- Do not name your employer or share any employer data.

## Feedback form (paste into Google Forms)
Intro text: "Thanks for trying CategoryOS (synthetic demo data only). Please don't enter confidential company data. Answers may be quoted only if you tick permission below."

1. What is your role and what type of company? (short text; e.g. "Category Manager, mid-size beauty D2C")
2. Which task in CategoryOS would save you the most time in your own week? (multiple choice: vendor onboarding / deal and offer evaluation / catalog QC / price-increase requests / weekly MIS and scorecards / promotions / competitor price watch / PO follow-up / other)
3. Roughly how much time would it save you, per week or per occurrence? (short text; e.g. "3 hrs/week", and what you would still do by hand)
4. In one or two lines, what's your honest reaction, including what you would not trust it with or what's missing? (paragraph)
5. May we quote your answer to Q4 with your name, role and company type? (multiple choice: Yes, by name / Yes, anonymised as "category manager at an e-commerce company" / No) and your name (short text, optional).
